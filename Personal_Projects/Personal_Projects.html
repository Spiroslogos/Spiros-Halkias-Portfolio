Home Lab (Mini)

I am running a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B 4GB RAM with a 128GB microSD as my first server. The case I use for cooling (and looks), is the Flirc Raspberry Pi 4 Case. It has a variety of functions that are listed below, with the name of the application, and what I use it for:

Glances – Monitoring dashboard for Raspberry Pi. Shows temperature, RAM, processes, etc.

Vaultwarden – Password and private information manager

Pi-hole – Network-wide Ad-blocker with private domain name system

TigerVNC – Remote SSH desktop of my Raspberry Pi to access the headless hardware

WireGuard – VPN that allows access to my Raspberry Pi and the rest of these applications via any network around the world

Little Armies

In Little Armies, you command a tiny battalion fighting for dominance where every troop counts. Each player leads their own army, playing battle cards to manipulate the battlefield. Will your little army rise to glory, or will it crumble under pressure?

The second board game I have ever made, Little Armies was made to compliment Ship Game, as a party game with room for more players and an easier floor for entry. The group of friends playtesting Ship Game grew too large, and I wanted everyone to play and have fun in only one game.

However, playing a board game with 10 people was a little more unstable than I thought. Images to be added.

Ship Game

Ship Game is a four-player strategy game where you each control a fleet of ships and battle to destroy your opponent's Moons while protecting your own. This turn based game starts off peaceful, but quickly ends up in chaos...

I was always into board games, and created my own rules for multiple board games I would play with friends and family. I took it one step further and decided to create my own board game, with everything from the games I loved.
